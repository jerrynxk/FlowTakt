1. 我们收集什么

FlowTakt 是一款本地优先的效率工具。我们（开发者）不收集、不上传、不存储任何您的个人身份信息或行为数据到我们自己的服务器。

  • 您创建的全部内容（任务、子任务、习惯、计时记录、专注记录、日程）仅保存在您设备本地。
  • 若您登录了 Apple ID 并开启同步，数据会通过 Apple iCloud（CloudKit）在您自己的设备之间加密同步。开发者无法读取这些内容。
  • 应用不含任何第三方 SDK、广告或分析库（零第三方依赖）。您的数据不会被出售或共享给任何第三方。

2. 崩溃与诊断

若您在系统设置中开启「共享 iPhone 与 iPad 分析」，相关匿名设备诊断信息由 Apple 收集，不经过开发者服务器，开发者无法关联到您个人。

3. 通知

应用使用系统本地通知实现专注提醒与周报推送，所有通知均在设备本地生成，不涉及网络传输。

4. 儿童

FlowTakt 不专门针对 13 岁以下儿童设计，但内容无任何年龄限制或不当信息，符合一般生产力工具定位。

5. 您的权利

由于数据完全存储在您自己的设备上，您可随时在应用内删除单条记录或通过「设置 → 重置数据」清空全部本地数据；卸载应用即移除全部本地数据。

如对本政策有疑问，请邮件联系：dlyzh_0303@qq.com

FlowTakt · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09 · For the iOS app FlowTakt (com.flowtakt.FlowTakt)

1. What we collect

FlowTakt is a local-first productivity app. We (the developer) do not collect, upload, or store any personally identifiable or behavioral data on our own servers.

  • All content you create (tasks, subtasks, habits, timers, focus sessions, schedules) is stored locally on your device only.
  • If you sign in with your Apple ID and enable sync, data is end-to-end synced across your own devices via Apple iCloud (CloudKit). The developer cannot read this content.
  • The app ships no third-party SDKs, ads, or analytics (zero third-party dependencies). Your data is never sold or shared with any third party.

2. Crashes & diagnostics

If you enable "Share iPhone & iPad Analytics" in system settings, anonymous device diagnostic data is collected by Apple, not by the developer's servers, and cannot be linked to you.

3. Notifications

The app uses on-device local notifications for focus reminders and weekly reports. No network transmission is involved.

4. Children

FlowTakt is not specifically directed to children under 13, but contains no age-restricted or inappropriate content.

5. Your rights

Because all data resides on your device, you may delete individual records in-app or wipe all local data via "Settings → Reset Data". Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
