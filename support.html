常见问题

iCloud 同步没生效？

请确认：① 设备已登录同一 Apple ID；② 系统「设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud → 显示全部」中已开启本 App 的同步开关；③ 网络正常。同步为 Apple 服务器行为，首次可能需数分钟。

后台计时 / 锁屏提醒不弹？

请允许 App 的「通知」权限（设置 → 通知 → FlowTakt → 允许通知）。计时在后台由系统本地通知驱动，无需保持前台。

如何归档已完成任务？

在「任务」页切换至「归档」标签，或在任务编辑中选择归档。归档后不从数据库中删除，可随时恢复。

数据保存在哪里？安全吗？

全部数据仅存于您设备本地，并可经 iCloud 在您自己的设备间加密同步。我们不上传任何数据到开发者服务器，详见隐私政策。

支持哪些系统？

最低 iOS 16.0，支持 iPhone 与 iPad，并适配深色模式与动态字体。

FAQ

iCloud sync isn't working?

Check: ① same Apple ID on devices; ② "Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Show All" has the app's sync toggle on; ③ network is available. Sync is handled by Apple's servers and may take a few minutes initially.

Background timer / lock-screen alerts don't appear?

Please allow Notifications for FlowTakt (Settings → Notifications → FlowTakt → Allow). Timers run via on-device local notifications and don't require the app to stay in the foreground.

How do I archive completed tasks?

Switch to the "Archived" tab on the Tasks screen, or choose archive in task edit. Archived items are not deleted and can be restored anytime.

Where is my data stored? Is it safe?

All data stays on your device and is end-to-end synced across your own devices via iCloud. We upload nothing to our servers. See the Privacy Policy.

Which systems are supported?

Requires iOS 16.0 or later. Works on iPhone and iPad, with Dark Mode and Dynamic Type support.
